What makes your model different
I would create a proprietary Crown Score (0–100) that's more than just home run probability.
Example:
Crown Score

40% Home Run Probability
20% Contact Quality
15% Matchup Advantage
10% Weather
10% Ballpark
5% Recent Form
Users would see:
👑 Crown Score: 96.8
⚾ HR Probability: 31.4%
💎 Value Edge: +7.2%
🔥 Confidence: A+
This gives them a clear ranking, an actual probability, and an indication of whether the sportsbook odds offer value.
Future enhancement
Once the model is working, you can retrain it every morning after games finish by ingesting the latest Statcast data. That allows the model to adapt over the course of the season as player performance, pitch mixes, and environmental factors change, keeping predictions current instead of relying on fixed historical relationships.
